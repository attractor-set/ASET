# ASET extension registry

This registry is informational and non-normative. An entry does not expand the active Seed canon and does not grant semantic precedence.

| Extension | Repository | Version | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| ASET Network Extension | `attractor-set/aset-network-extension` | `0.1.0-alpha.1` | Federation topology and cross-context local recognition | Published alpha |

Every extension must pin an exact Seed canon package, preserve all Seed invariants, publish its own invariants and portable conformance cases, and remain independently versioned.
