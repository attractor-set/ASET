# Authority and responsibility

Authority is an active Hohfeldian POWER bound to one Context, capability and non-overlapping scope. Namespace ancestry, possession of a key, API access or a role label do not create Authority.

Permit issuance requires both an issuer Decision and delegate readiness bound to the same PermitTerms digest. Authority transfer additionally requires responsibility readiness signed by the new holder and a positive local Outcome for the exact transfer task. The entire action lineage must share the Authority Context.

Withdrawn, superseded and terminated Contexts cannot retain active Authority or active Permit. Historical responsibility is append-only and is not transferred retroactively.
