# Canonicality

`seed/canonical/` is the active normative ASET Seed resolution core. Its machine-readable model, referenced protocol schemas, requirements, invariants, conformance corpus and package identity define the active canon.

The active decision lattice is `UNKNOWN -> ACCEPT | DENY`. `UNKNOWN` is semantically distinct from `DENY` and remains operationally blocked. A next Resolution Authority is valid only through an explicit escalation grant.

No implementation, extension, programming language, storage engine or checker has semantic precedence. Unreferenced rc11/rc12 material and component canons are historical or extension migration inputs and do not expand the active Seed.

Active Seed CI, formal assurance, release gating and implementation-profile notification have no
Monade or component-assurance dependency. The preserved `aset/` tree is noncontrolling historical
migration evidence pending extraction and is not included in the Seed canon package.
